Privacy policy

Effective 22 September 2026. Applies to ddelmoli's private tools that connect to the author's own accounts. It does not cover the other projects on ddelmoli.github.io, some of which are public.

Who uses them

Only its author. These tools are not offered to anyone else, and they have no other users.

What they access

Only the author's own accounts and data, with the author's permission, and no more access than each tool needs.

What they collect

Nothing from anyone other than the author. Data stays under the author's control and is processed only by services the author chooses to use. It is never sold, and never used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access granted to any of these tools can be withdrawn at any time from the account's own security settings, for example myaccount.google.com/permissions for a Google account.
